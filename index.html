03 / Behind the screen

Two paths to discovery.

The same movie catalog can look different when we change the information used to make recommendations.

Task 1 · Content-based

Start with the movie itself

I compare information such as genre, plot, director, and cast. In my notebook, TF-IDF and cosine similarity measure relationships between movie descriptions and other metadata.

Movie metadata→TF-IDF→Similarity
Task 2 · Collaborative

Look at rating patterns

IMDb Extensive provides average ratings for age-and-gender cohorts. My notebook compares those patterns without pretending they are the rating histories of identifiable people.

Cohort averages→Pearson similarity→Group estimates
The on-page demo uses simplified calculations on fictional records. The notebook uses TF-IDF and a cohort-level rating method on real IMDb CSV files after the dataset is attached. A cohort average is not a personal rating.
