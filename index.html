TASK 01 / IMDb EXTENSIVE

Content-Based Filtering

I turn movie genres, descriptions, directors and actors into numerical text features. I then compare films by cosine similarity to identify related titles.

Movie metadata→TF-IDF→Cosine similarity
TASK 02 / IMDb EXTENSIVE

Collaborative cohort approximation

I study six demographic rating cohorts, compare group patterns with Pearson correlation and evaluate held-out group-average estimates. These are not personalized viewer predictions.

Cohort averages→Pearson→MAE / RMSE
